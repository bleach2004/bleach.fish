through a nausea of currents
i catch my drift

displaced organs and tightened muscles left me buoyant and exhausted
an eager wave takes its turn to rise
and perform its dance for the next in line
puppeteered by flurrying winds
earth's pressure showing through erosion of my jaw

but as the storm begins to lull
sporadic gasps make rhythmic breaths
through a nausea of currents
i catch my drift
weathering old flesh

one stroke at a time
